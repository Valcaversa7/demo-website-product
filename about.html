Our workshop

Built on honest grading.

We make quality technology more affordable. From carefully tested second-hand devices to powerful custom PC builds, we help customers get the performance they need without overspending.

How a device gets listed

Intake, function test, cosmetic grade, photograph, price. If it fails a test, it does not go on the site — it goes to parts or repair. That is the whole model.

01 Intake

Identity, storage wipe, and a written condition note before anything is powered for long.

02 Test

Displays, batteries, cameras, ports, thermals, and storage health — logged against the SKU.

03 Grade

Excellent, Very Good, or Good. We describe marks in plain language, not auction-speak.

04 Warranty

90 days on pre-owned, one year on custom builds. After-sales is a person, not a ticket maze.

Grading

Second-hand quality system

Excellent

Minimal signs of use

Looks close to new. No dents, no screen marks — functionally indistinguishable from retail.

Very Good

Light cosmetic wear

Light scuffs or keyboard shine. Nothing that affects use or photos from a normal distance.

Good

Visible signs of use, fully functional

Honest wear. Every port and test still passes. The value pick when performance matters more than cosmetics.
